# 12bajay workout: design brief
Job: log sets and meals fast, one-handed, mid-workout. Audience: one person training at a gym or home in India.
Reference: quiet logging apps (Hevy, Apple Fitness rings): content on the page, few boxes, big tap targets.
## Tokens
- Accent: one colour, #0E7C66 (dark mode #34C3A0). Ink #16201C, paper #F3F5F2, line #DDE2DC.
- Font: Bricolage Grotesque, 400-800.
- Spacing: 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 (--s1 to --s6).
- Radius: 10px everywhere (--r).
- Motion: only for responses to taps (sheet, menu, dock indicator, set check). Off under reduced-motion.
## Copy rules
Sentence case, plain verbs, errors say what happened and what to do, empty states say the next action.
